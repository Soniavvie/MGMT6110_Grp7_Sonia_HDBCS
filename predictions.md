## 2. My findings

### Finding 1
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the results page after an evaluation, in the Recent Comparable Transactions list.
- What I did, what I saw: I evaluated a flat and read the comparable transactions. They are dated in the year 2000, although the list is labelled "recent" and the asking price I entered is a 2026 price.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: System. The page can only show the transactions it is sent, and the function in api/ is sending ones from 2000, so the screen cannot fix this on its own.
- Severity, and why: 4, driven by damage to the product's standing. Comparing a 2026 price with 2000 sales gives a confidently wrong verdict and gap figure, which defeats the one job the product does, and anyone who notices stops trusting it.
- The repair: The comparables come from the most recent months of resale data, and the page states the period they cover and when the data was last updated.

### Finding 2
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the results page, at the heading "Recent Comparable Transactions (2000)".
- What I did, what I saw: I read the heading and could not tell whether 2000 is the number of transactions or the year they come from.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already has both the count and the dates, and only the way it labels them is unclear.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by how often it happens. Every visitor reads the heading, but the list below it lets most of them work out the meaning.
- The repair: The heading says in words what the number is, for example "20 transactions" or "from 2000", so it cannot be read two ways.

### Finding 3
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the results page, in the Recent Comparable Transactions list.
- What I did, what I saw: I looked down the list of transactions. They appear in no order by date, so the most recent sale is not at the top.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already has the date of each transaction and can put them in order itself.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by the cost when it happens. A visitor can still read every row, but has to hunt for the latest sales, which matter most for pricing.
- The repair: Transactions appear newest first, so the list reads as "recent" does.

### Finding 4
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the evaluation form, in the asking price box.
- What I did, what I saw: I tried to delete the 0 in the asking price box before typing my price. Backspace would not remove it. Only highlighting the 0 and typing over it replaced it, and otherwise my number was added after the 0.
- Which heuristic: 5, Error Prevention.
- Screen or system: Screen. The box and its starting value live entirely on the page.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by how often it happens. Every visitor has to enter an asking price, and the stuck 0 leads straight into the failure in Finding 5.
- The repair: The asking price box starts empty, or its starting value can be deleted as usual, and what the visitor types is the whole price.

### Finding 5
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the evaluation form, after entering an asking price.
- What I did, what I saw: I entered 079000 as the asking price and ran the evaluation. Nothing happened, and nothing on screen said why.
- Which heuristic: 9, Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page itself decides the price is not acceptable, so it already knows enough to say so.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by the cost when it happens. The visitor gets no result and no reason, and may give up thinking the site is broken.
- The repair: A price with a leading zero is either accepted as the number it means, or the page says beside the box what is wrong and what to enter instead.

### Finding 6
- Where: https://mgmy6110grp7soniahdbcs.vercel.app/, on the results page after evaluating a flat in Bukit Timah at an asking price of $700,000.
- What I did, what I saw: I chose Bukit Timah and entered $700,000. The results did not show the statistics, and nothing said they were unavailable or why.
- Which heuristic: 9, Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page knows it received no comparable transactions, or too few, and that is enough to say so.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by damage to the product's standing. In towns with few resale flats, a blank result looks like a broken product rather than a lack of data.
- The repair: When there are not enough comparable sales, the page says so plainly and suggests what to try, such as another flat type or a nearby town.
